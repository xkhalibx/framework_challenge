How Adoption Works

  1. Submit an Application: Fill out the inquiry form below telling us about your home and lifestyle.
  2. Virtual Chat: Our volunteer coordinator will call within 48 hours to discuss your match.
  3. Meet & Greet: Visit our shelter or foster home to meet your prospective pup in person.
  4. Take Your Best Friend Home: Complete the $175 adoption fee (covers vaccinations, microchip, and spay/neuter).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the adoption fee?

All dogs are spayed or neutered, up-to-date on core vaccinations, heartworm tested, and microchipped prior to going home.

Can I adopt if I live in an apartment?

Yes! Many of our dogs thrive in apartments as long as they receive regular daily walks and mental enrichment.

Do you offer foster-to-adopt trials?

We offer a 14-day foster-to-adopt trial for households with existing dogs to ensure everyone gets along comfortably.
